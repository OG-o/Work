# 📱 Android & Termux Beginner's Guide to Ubuntu Cloud PC

A complete, beginner-friendly guide to running and using your Ubuntu Cloud PC on an Android phone using Termux.

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Quick Launch from Termux

Open **Termux** on your Android phone and run this single command:

```bash
pkg update -y && pkg install -y git curl
git clone https://github.com/OG-o/Work.git
cd Work
./setup.sh
```

Once the script finishes, it will print your **Live Web Access URL**:
```
👉 https://xxxx-xxxx-xxxx.trycloudflare.com
```

---

## 🌐 Step 2: Open the Desktop on Your Phone

1. Copy the **Live URL** printed by the setup script.
2. Open **Google Chrome** (or any browser) on your Android phone.
3. Paste the URL and hit enter.
4. Your full **Ubuntu 24.04 Desktop** will immediately load on your phone screen!

---

## 📱 Step 3: Install as an App on Your Home Screen (1-Tap Launch)

Turn your cloud desktop into a real full-screen Android app:

1. In Google Chrome on your phone, tap the **3 vertical dots menu (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
2. Tap **"Install App"** (or **"Add to Home screen"**).
3. Confirm by tapping **Install**.
4. An app icon named **"Cloud PC"** will now appear on your phone's home screen!
5. Whenever you tap the icon, your desktop opens in **full-screen mode** with no browser address bar!

---

## ⌨️ Step 4: How to Type on Your Phone

Look at the **top-right corner** of your screen for the floating toolbar:

| Button | How to Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`[ ⌨️ Keyboard ]`** | Tap this button to immediately open your phone's keyboard (Gboard / Samsung Keyboard). |
| **`[ 💬 Type/Paste ]`** | Tap this to paste long text, website URLs, or code directly into the active window. |
| **`[ 🔍 Fit ]`** | Tap this to auto-zoom and fit the full desktop to your phone screen perfectly. |
| **On-Screen Keyboard** | A virtual touch keyboard is also docked at the bottom of the desktop for direct finger taps. |

---

## 🔊 Step 5: How to Hear Sound on Your Phone

1. Tap the **`[ 🔊 Audio ]`** button in the top-right toolbar.
2. The button turns green (**`🔊 Audio: ON`**).
3. Play any video on YouTube in Chrome or songs in VLC — the sound will play directly out of your phone's speakers!

---

## 💻 Step 6: Available Apps on Your Desktop

- **Visual Studio Code & Antigravity IDE**: Full code editor with extensions and terminal.
- **Google Chrome**: Full web browser (zero crashes).
- **BlueStacks Android Player**: Play Android games and apps on PC.
- **Ubuntu Terminal**: Complete command line with `sudo` root privileges.
- **Thunar File Manager**: Browse, organize, and extract zip files.
